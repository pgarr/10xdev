## What

Build the complete v1 guest team analyzer: autocomplete Pokémon search, per-selection PokeAPI data fetch (cached at the Cloudflare edge), a two-component scoring engine, and a single-view results panel — all without login.

## Why

S-01 is the north star: the only user story and the sole primary Success Criterion. Everything else in the product only matters if this works and casual players find it more useful than raw type data.
